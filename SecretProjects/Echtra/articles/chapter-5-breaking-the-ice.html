Chapter 5: Breaking the Ice

The thaw and the Fomorian invasion

  • Adventure Chapter

With the Heart of Winter shattered, Irgaelia begins to thaw. The melting Ever-Frozen Sea opens a route for the Fomorians, who launch an invasion and attack the tribes while the people are still recovering from winter.

The party must help organize resistance, answer King Nuada's task and travel toward Raven's Keep. The chapter includes the Griffon's Crossing, Sligeach Forest and the ruins beneath Raven's Keep.

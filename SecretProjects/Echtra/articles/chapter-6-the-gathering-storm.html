Chapter 6: The Gathering Storm

The counteroffensive, alliances and voyage west

  • Adventure Chapter

The Fomorian invasion is repelled, but the tribes recognize that another attack is possible. The party is charged with preparing a counteroffensive against the Fomorian homeland.

The preparation involves choosing a vessel, assembling a crew, seeking Manannán's blessing and building broader alliances before sailing west.

Chapter 3: The Longest Night

The Bear, Raven and Snake tribes and the Long Winter

  • Adventure Chapter

The Long Winter has begun. The sun barely rises, and the party must gather the Archdruids of Irgaelia for a Comairle. The chapter moves through the Bear, Raven and Snake tribes, each contributing locations, NPCs, threats and clues.

The chapter includes the Darkwood, a time-looped glade, Deirdre's troubled sleep, the Nightmare Realm, the Heartwood Elm and the source of a curse, culminating in the council and its revelations.

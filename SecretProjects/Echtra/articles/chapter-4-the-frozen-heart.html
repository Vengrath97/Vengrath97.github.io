Chapter 4: The Frozen Heart

Ever-Frozen Sea, Cailleach and the Heart of Winter

  • Adventure Chapter

The Song of the Everfrost reveals that the unnatural winter flows from the Heart of Winter, an artifact hidden in the Lands of Always-Winter. The party must cross the Ever-Frozen Sea and confront Cailleach's servants.

The chapter culminates in a confrontation with Caoránach, Cailleach's chosen champion and guardian of the Heart. Destroying the Heart breaks the supernatural winter, but the thaw exposes consequences that the ice had kept contained.

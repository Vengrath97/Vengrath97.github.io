Tribes of Irgaelia

Tuath na Comhdhála

The Tribe of the Gathering, seated in Comairle. Patron Spirit: Owl. Important Figures: King Nuada, Queen Boyne

As the tribe that hosts the sacred meeting grounds of Comairle, the Comhdhála have long served as neutral arbiters between the other tribes. Their patron owl symbolizes the wisdom required to navigate the complex politics of Irgaelia, and it is said that owls nest in great numbers around the Yew of Wisdom. Unlike other tribes led by chieftains, the Comhdhála are ruled by a king and queen—a tradition dating back to when the first Comairle was called.

Tuath na Trá Fuara

The Tribe of the Cold Shore, seated in Cúilfeorna. Patron Spirit: Wolf. Important Figures: Chieftain Fionn Halfhand, Druid Keegan mac Éamoinn

The northernmost tribe, the Trá Fuara are a hardy people who endure the harshest winters and fiercest storms. Their wolf spirit embodies both the fierce pack loyalty that binds them and their reputation as relentless hunters. Fionn Halfhand earned his name—and the respect of his people—by losing three fingers to frostbite during a legendary táin (raid) into the frozen wastes, yet continuing to lead his warriors to victory.

Tuath na Gréine Éirí

The Tribe of the Rising Sun, seated in Eornaigh. Patron Spirit: Bear. Important Figures: Chieftain Eoghan Brightblade, Druid Aodhán mac Ríogháin

Dwelling on the eastern coast where dawn first touches Irgaelia, the Gréine Éirí revere the bear for its strength and protective nature. They are known as fierce warriors who fight with the rising sun at their backs, believing it grants them divine favor. Their lands border the mysterious Darkwood, and they maintain ancient pacts with some of its stranger inhabitants.

Tuath na Farraige Te

The Tribe of the Warm Ocean, seated in Suidheach. Patron Spirit: Snake. Important Figures: Chieftain Caoimhín Swiftwater, Druid Róisín mac Fhearchair

The Farraige Te make their home in the southern coastal regions where warm currents keep the waters temperate year-round. The serpent spirit represents transformation, wisdom, and the flowing nature of water—traits these people embody. They are master sailors and traders, their serpent-prowed ships a common sight along Irgaelia's coasts. The Great Serpent's Hollow at Suidheach is said to reach down to the primordial depths where the first serpents still sleep.

Tuath na hAbhann Síobtha

The Tribe of the Flowing River, seated in Airthin. Patron Spirit: Raven. Important Figures: Chieftain Fiachra Wavecaller, Druid Deirdre mac Néill

The hAbhann Síobtha control the western coastal territories where swift rivers meet the sea. Their raven spirit is both an omen-bearer and a psychopomp, guiding souls between worlds—fitting for a people who live where river and ocean, fresh and salt, constantly mix. They have close ties to Manannán mac Lir and are renowned for their weather-workers who can call storms or calm seas. The Tidepools of Nechtan on their shores are considered sacred.

Tuath na Cloiche Ársa

The Tribe of the Ancient Stone, seated in Silverna. Patron Spirit: Stag. Important Figures: Chieftain Conall Stoneguard, Druid Síle mac Conchobhair

In the highland territories dotted with ancient standing stones and megalithic monuments, the Cloiche Ársa maintain the old ways with unwavering devotion. Their stag spirit represents nobility, connection to the sacred landscape, and the endurance of stone itself. They are the keepers of many forgotten rituals and serve as historians for deeds carved in stone circles across Irgaelia. Some whisper that their chieftains can hear the stones speak.

Tuath na Taoide Sníofa

The Tribe of the Woven Tides, seated in Clachair. Patron Spirit: Dolphin. Important Figures: Chieftain Domhnall Tidewalker, Druid Fionnuala mac Ruaidhrí

The Taoide Sníofa inhabit the southern archipelagos where the tides weave intricate patterns between countless islands. Their dolphin spirit embodies intelligence, playfulness, and the ability to navigate complex currents—both literal and political. They are masterful boat-builders and have developed a unique culture that lives as much on water as on land. It's said that dolphins guide their ships through the most treacherous passages, and that some tribe members can hold their breath for impossible lengths of time.

Tuath na Lasracha Síoraí

The Tribe of the Eternal Flame, seated in Feithra. Patron Spirit: Lion. Important Figures: Chieftain Tadhg Fireborn, Druid Bríghid mac Eoghain

The Lasracha Síoraí guard the volcanic highlands of the southeast, where geothermal springs bubble and ancient lava flows have created a unique landscape. Their lion spirit represents courage, the eternal flame of inspiration, and the creative fire that forges civilization. They are renowned smiths and artisans, their works bearing the mark of both flame and passion. The sacred flame in Feithra has burned continuously for over three hundred years, tended by Brigid's devotees.

Tuath na Gaoithe Cogarnaí

The Tribe of the Whispering Winds, seated in Ardin. Patron Spirit: Eagle. Important Figures: Chieftain Niall Windwhisper, Druid Aoife mac Séamais

High in the mountain valleys where winds carry voices across vast distances, the Gaoithe Cogarnaí have learned to read messages in every gust and gale. Their eagle spirit soars above all, seeing far and wide, representing vision, freedom, and connection to the heavens. They are master scouts and messengers, and their warriors train to fight on precarious mountain paths where one misstep means death. The winds around Ardin are said to whisper prophecies to those who know how to listen.

Adventure Overview

The story starts with a prologue, that is being followed by 4 acts, each representing a season of the year. Each of these acts focuses on a different looming threat, and requires the heroes to be involved to resolve them, and each of the acts change the weather around them - and with weather, the themes change along.

Morrigan, the Phantom Queen, is plotting to use the Wild Hunt to conquer the mortal realm. She manipulates the Dullahan into creating a prophecy that she will later fulfill. This prophecy sets in motion a series of events, beginning with an unnatural winter that the characters must understand and overcome. As they seek answers, the heroes are guided by druids who lead them on a journey to destroy the Heart of Winter, a powerful artifact created by Morrigan’s ally, Caillach, the Goddess of Winter. When the Heart is destroyed, the frozen sea melts, allowing the Fomorians to invade. The party then sails to the Fomorian lands to confront their leader, Balor, who is manipulated by Morrigan into waging war.

However, even after defeating the Fomorians, the world remains in darkness. The Sun refuses to rise, and the druids reveal that the Summer Queen, a member of the Seelie Court, has been imprisoned by the Autumn King to prevent the return of summer and trap the world in endless twilight. The characters free the Summer Queen, but even after her liberation, the world remains in chaos, with factions on the brink of war. As the Wild Hunt approaches, the heroes must face Morrigan in her domain and end her plans once and for all.

Act Overview

The story is divided into a prologue and five acts:

Prologue:

As the campaign starts with the beginning of the Year, at Samhain, the Prologue establishes Dullahan as the Harbringer of Doom: he takes one life, but announces that the Wild Hunt will take all lives before the next Samhain.

Act I: Winter

Winter begins, and it is not ordinary. Cailleach, goddess of Winter seeks to expand her domain. A supernatural winter is brought to Irgaelia, and the heroes must find a way for the people to survive, and fend off the encrouching cold.

Act II: Spring

As the ice melts, the people of Irgaelia look with hope to the future - only for the land to be invaded by opportunistic Fomorians, and their king. The heroes must garner help from the Tuath (tribes) and repel the monstrous invaders.

Act III: Summer

As the triumph over Fomorians shines bright, so does the summer Sun should shine on the land - but it does not. It's light is bleak, and the heroes search and find their way to The Otherworld, to contact The Summer Queen - the queen of the Seelie Court, to bring back the sun. As it turns out, she had been abducted by the Autumn King - a leader of the Unseelie Court. The heroes must rescue her, to bring back the summer's warmth.

Act IV: Autumn

With autumn in full swing, the Wild Hunt descends upon Irgaelia, led by the Dullahan. The heroes are thrust into a relentless pursuit of souls as they strive to protect their home from this dark force.

Act V: The Full Wheel

As the omens of war rise, The Morrigan, goddess of War and Fate remains to be confronted, her manipulations of the factions of Irgaelia, discord she sew among tribes and the Seidhe Courts unveiled. The heroes must navigate the chaos, seeking alliances and gather allies in spite of The Morrigan's machinations.

Story Structure

Eachtra midst the Sidhe has been designed with natural ‘phases’ that shape the adventure until the players achieve certain milestones. New challenges and goals unlock in the following order:

• Attending the Samhain Festival... …beings The Harshest Winter

• Destroying the Frozen Heart... …thaws the frozen sea for Fomorian invasion

• Killing the Fomorian King …causes the sun to bleak

• Rescuing the Summer Queen …triggers the Wild Hunt

• Defeating Dullahan… …leads the heroes to confront Morrigan

Every band of heroes will explore Irgaelia differently and complete the plots in a variety of ways. As GM, you merely guide the party from one major milestone to another. Their decisions throughout determine how the Wild Hunt will begin.. and how will it end.

Chapter Overview

A short breakdown of the chapters:

C1: Samhain Festival

Hook: The adventure begins at the Samhain Festival. Amidst the celebration, a spectral rider—Dullahan—arrives, bringing an ominous prophecy. Main Goal: Survive the arrival of Dullahan and hear his prophecy that foretells the coming of the Wild Hunt in one year's time. Arc-Villain: Dullahan, the Harbinger of Doom.

C2: Comairle

Hook: Following Dullahan’s prophecy and the onset of a sudden, unnatural winter, the party is sent to gather the druids of Irgaelia for a council. Main Goal: Carry the news of the grim prophecy to King Nuada. Arc-Villain: None directly, though hints of darker forces influencing the winter.

C3: The Longest Night

Hook: The druids reveal that the land is under a curse, and the only way to lift it is by finding the Heart of Winter. The heroes are tasked with locating this mysterious source. Main Goal: Assemble the wise druids to seek their guidance on how to prevent the impending doom. Arc-Villain: Various winter-themed foes (frost giants, winter wolves, ice elementals) acting under Cailleach's influence.

C4: The Frozen Heart

Hook: The heroes discover that the Heart of Winter lies in the frozen north, where Cailleach, the Goddess of Winter, holds sway. They must confront her to end the eternal winter. Main Goal: Venture north to find and destroy the Heart of Winter, thereby weakening Cailleach's grip over the land. Arc-Villain: Cailleach, the Winter Goddess.

C5: The Breaking of the Seal

Hook: With the thawing of the snow, the melting ice weakens an ancient seal that held back the Fomorians. The monstrous invaders attack Irgaelia, seeking to reclaim their lost dominion. Main Goal: Defend Irgaelia from the sudden onslaught of Fomorian forces. Arc-Villain: Fomorian Warlords leading the initial assault.

C6: The Gathering Storm

Hook: Realizing the Fomorian threat is far greater than anticipated, the heroes are tasked with gathering allies, resources, and a flying ship to launch a counteroffensive. Main Goal: Secure alliances with other races and factions (dwarves, elves, dragons) and acquire a flying ship to take the fight to the Fomorian homeland. Arc-Villain: Agents of Balor seeking to sabotage the party's efforts.

C7: The Evil Eye

Hook: With a flying ship and newfound allies, the heroes embark on a mission to invade the Fomorian lands and confront their leader. Main Goal: Defeat Balor, the King of the Fomorians, and his dreaded Evil Eye to end the Fomorian threat. Arc-Villain: Balor, King of the Fomorians.

C8: The Dimming Sun

Hook: Despite their victory over the Fomorians, the sun fails to rise properly, leaving the world in a perpetual twilight. The druids reveal that the balance between the Seelie and Unseelie Courts is disturbed. Main Goal: Investigate the cause of the dimming sun and find the entrance to the mystical realm of the Sidhe. Arc-Villain: Dark Fey working for the Autumn King.

C9: The Sidhe

Hook: The heroes enter the Feywild and are thrust into the intricate politics of the Seelie and Unseelie Courts. They learn that the Summer Queen has been imprisoned by the Autumn King. Main Goal: Navigate Fey politics to gather allies and uncover a way to free the Summer Queen. Arc-Villain: The Autumn King, a manipulative ruler of the Unseelie Court.

C10: Rescue in Twilight

Hook: A daring plan is set in motion to infiltrate the Autumn King's Court and rescue the Summer Queen from her enchanted prison. Main Goal: Free the Summer Queen to restore the balance of the seasons and bring back the sun’s light. Arc-Villain: The Autumn King and his elite Fey guardians.

C11: Whispers of War

Hook: With the approach of autumn, whispers of dissent and rebellion grow among the human kingdoms, fueled by agents of the Morrigan. The heroes must prevent an all-out civil war. Main Goal: Uncover and thwart the Morrigan’s plot to incite war among mortals, weakening them before the Wild Hunt arrives. Arc-Villain: Morrigan’s mortal agents and corrupted nobles.

C12: The Phantom’s Grasp

Hook: The Morrigan reveals herself as the true mastermind, manipulating events from behind the scenes. Her influence spreads, causing plagues of undead and phantasmal horrors. Main Goal: Disrupt the Morrigan's dark rituals and sever her connection to the mortal realm, preventing her from gaining ultimate power. Arc-Villain: The Morrigan’s spectral minions and corrupted druids.

C13: The Wild Hunt

Hook: The prophecy comes to pass as the Wild Hunt rides across the land, led by Dullahan. The heroes must confront the spectral riders in a final bid to save Irgaelia. Main Goal: Defeat the Wild Hunt and discover Morrigan's involvement, turning Dullahan against her if possible. Arc-Villain: Dullahan, now revealed as the leader of the Wild Hunt under Morrigan’s command.

C14: War on the Phantom Queen

Hook: The final confrontation against the Morrigan takes place in her fortress beyond the mortal realm. The heroes must navigate a realm of shadows and confront the Phantom Queen herself. Main Goal: Defeat Morrigan, ending her reign of terror and preventing her from unleashing eternal chaos on the world. Arc-Villain: Morrigan, the Phantom Queen.
